# Drift

A private daily check-in that learns *your* normal week. You log a few numbers once a day: sleep and wake times, screen time, late-night use, scrolling apps, steps, how many people you texted back, and a 1-tap mood. After 5 days, Drift compares each day with your own baseline (the median of your last 28 days). When several signals move together it suggests one small thing to do, and it learns which suggestions you actually do.

- **Your data stays on your device.** Everything is saved in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded. You can export, import or delete it from the Check-in tab.
- **Circle:** add up to 3 trusted people. On a day well off your normal, Drift can open a short, kind text in your own phone's messages app. You press send, and no data is shared.
- **Calm helper:** a free AI chat (no key, no account) for grounding exercises and help drafting a text. Only what you type into the chat is sent to the free AI service.
- **Crisis:** one tap to call or text 988, or to call 911.
- Add a daily reminder to your calendar (.ics) and install Drift to your home screen (PWA).

**Personal, non-clinical use only.** Drift is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything. Using it with patients or clients, or connecting it to real health records, would need a HIPAA review first. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US).

It's a single static file: `index.html`.
